Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Alexon Home Services (“Alexon”, “we”) is a home services business in Edmonton, Alberta. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it, in line with Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your neighbourhood, postal code or address, so we can come to the job.
  • Details and photos of the work you need.
  • Records of the work we do for you, such as estimates and invoices.

How we collect it

You give it to us when you call or text us, or when you use the “Let us call you” form. The form does not send anything to a server: it opens your messaging app with a text addressed to us, and nothing is sent until you press send.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your request and give you a price.
  • To schedule and do the work.
  • For invoices, warranty follow-up and the records the law requires us to keep.

We don’t use your information for anything else without asking you first, and we never sell or rent it.

Calls and texts

We contact you only about your request or your job. If you’d rather not get texts, just tell us.

Sharing

We share information only when it’s needed to do your job — for example, a delivery address for materials — or when the law requires it.

This website

The site itself doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the home page shows a map from Google Maps, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads and the map may set Google’s own cookies (see Google’s Privacy Policy).

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for the reasons above. Business records such as invoices are kept for six years, as the Canada Revenue Agency requires.

Keeping it safe

We keep your information on password-protected devices and in our own records, and only Alexon uses it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent (this may mean we can’t finish a job). Call or text us at 780-280-2404. If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta at oipc.ab.ca.

Contact

Privacy questions: Alexander, Alexon Home Services, 780-280-2404.
